# Fishing v2 (v1.0.603)

Heads-up for sarunphat: the fishing minigame screen is replaced, and the round flow changes a little. Shared files touched: `camp-system/camp-socket.js` (fishing routes), `camp-system/camp-constants.js` (two new messages), `module.json` (one new script). The cast → reel core loop is the same, and failing is still possible.

Approved mockup (v20): https://claude.ai/artifact/JWMB2X6uGUZJZWn4BjKNpv

## What changes at the table (Oni's rulings, 2026-11-13)

- **Tackle first.** Each round opens on a Tackle window (wood/parchment style): a **rod** (Plain Rod + any fishing rod the angler carries) and a **bait** (Bare Hook + any material the angler carries, the Cooking idea). The bait's taste picks its effect and its rarity sets the strength:
  - umami: the cast counts higher toward the fish's rank
  - sweet: fish come sooner and take the hook more often
  - salty: better chest chance
  - sour: calmer fish
  - bitter: this area's own fish bite more
  - a caught fish: bites one rank higher, but the fish fights harder
  - no taste: plain bait
  One bait is used per cast.
- **Cast** as before, now with rank marks on the gauge and a sound per cast level.
- **Lure.** The bait bobs and the underwater line sways while it waits. Fish visit one at a time. Each one either looks and leaves, nibbles and leaves, or nibbles and gets hooked, shown by a **"!"** bubble. Strike with Space, Z, Enter or a click. Striking on the closing ring gives **Nice** (±130 ms, meter starts at 45%) or **Perfect** (±70 ms, 65%); a normal strike starts at 40%. If the player doesn't strike, the fish shakes free and the lure goes on.
  - Lure time: 20 s at a camp activity and a dungeon Fishing tile. A map fishing spot has no limit, and Esc reels in for a fresh cast.
  - The fish stays a silhouette (3 shapes, size by rank) until the result window.
- **Fight.** Same bar and meter, plus:
  - fights by rank (×0.75 / 0.9 / 1.05 / 1.25)
  - Keren fix: the bar always rises at least 110
  - DEX nimble bar: +5% bar speed both ways per DEX point above 8
  - WLP: +1.5 s per point above 8
  - fights run a little longer (fill ×0.85, drain ×0.75, +2 s)
  - a **sunken chest** shows up in 20% of fights (salty bait adds more), in one of three timing patterns. It's kept only if the fish lands (Stardew rule).
- **Result window** (wood/parchment): the fish revealed, its rank and price, **NEW!** on the first catch by this character, **PERFECT +1** (the fish never left the bar; not for Legendary), and the chest reward. Hovering shows the game's loot tooltips. Between rounds the owner presses CONTINUE.
- **Rods**: four key items in the new **Tackle** folder (Ironwood MIG, Swiftline DEX, Seer's INS, Stillwater WLP). Each makes its attribute count one die size higher while fishing. Nobody has them yet: Oni decides later where players get them.

## Code

- `camp-system/activities/fishing-tuning.js` (new, before `activity-fishing.js` in module.json) holds every number and the pure rules as `CampSystem.FishingTuning`, plus the pixel silhouette painter. `fishing-tuning.test.mjs`: 76 checks, including a draw-call-for-draw-call diff against the mockup's painter (`FISH_MOCKUP=<template> node …`).
- `activity-fishing-ui.js`: **replaced** (port of the mockup).
  - Public API kept: `show`, `spectateBegin`, `beginRound`, `onCast`, `onHit`, `applyResult`, `resolveRound`, `resolveProceed`, `hide`.
  - New: `onLure`, `resolveContinue`, `touch`, `_debug()`.
  - The overlay id is still `oni-camp-fish-ovl` (`fishing-spot.js` checks it).
  - All CSS is scoped under that id.
  - Spectators mirror every phase.
  - An actor with no player owner plays on the **primary** GM only (before, every GM client ran it as owner).
- `activity-fishing.js` (GM side):
  - The pools carry each fish's picture, shape, size, liked taste and an "this area's own fish" mark.
  - The round timeout is now **2 minutes of no message from the angler**, so an unlimited lure stays alive while the player is active.
  - The automatic 2.2 s pause between rounds is replaced by the owner's CONTINUE.
  - Perfect catch awards quantity 2.
  - Chest loot is rolled on the area's own loot tables (Dungeon Configuration, the same ones the loot tiles use). "It's Empty" / "Junk" rows re-roll, the IP table is skipped while the angler's IP is full, and the reward is awarded straight to the angler through `oni.TreasureRoulette.AwardDispatcher.award`, with no recipient screen.
  - The chat summary lists chest loot.
- `camp-socket.js`:
  - `FISHING_START` passes its whole payload to `show()`.
  - `FISHING_BEGIN` passes the payload.
  - New `FISHING_LURE` (owner → all).
  - `FISHING_RESULT` → `applyResult(..., payload)`.
  - The owner's result goes to `resolveRound` whole.
  - New `FISHING_CONTINUE` (owner → primary GM).
- Callers (`camp-ui-activity-resolve`, `tile-event-fishing`, `fishing-spot`) are unchanged. The lure limit comes from `source: "spot"` (0) versus anything else (20).
