   Damage (Dagger) + Damage (Sword). The enemy's weapon read still sees a dagger.

## Known
- `move-engine pack` is slow: about 7 minutes per battle (full canvas, needed for the damage numbers). Run it after a
  change you want numbers for, not on every slider move.
- The isolated compute harnesses (`runDirectorSkillCompute` / attack compute) return 0 damage for carrier attacks; the
  pack uses real sim battles instead.
- The stale flag compares every bound slider, including ones that don't change damage (MP cost).
