# Hako Chapter 4 stock + Doom + Weighted (branch feat/hako-ch4, not pushed)

Heads-up for sarunphat: two rules change for **every** creature, not just Hako's gear.

1. **Doom now kills.** The library "Doomed" status counts down at the afflicted creature's turn start (3 charges, a "Doom draws near…" card each tick) and deals lethal damage on the last tick. Before this it was a label that did nothing. Death-saves (Life Charm etc.) still get their chance because the hit goes through the normal damage path. Any monster skill that applies "Doomed" is now really lethal.
2. **Weighted is enforced.** One attack per turn with a Weighted weapon, and no attacks without High Roll with it (free attacks with HR 0, two-weapon modes). Player menus stamp the blocked rows; NPC autopilot drops them. Weapons that carry `weighted` after the migration: Death Scythe, Reaper Scythe, Giant Club, Morning Star, Minotaurus Axe, Golem Fist, Mountain Cleaver, Gae Bolg; Titanic Shield grants it to the equipped weapon.
